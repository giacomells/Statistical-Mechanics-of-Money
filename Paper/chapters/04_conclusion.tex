\section{Conclusion}

In the absence of detailed knowledge of the microscopic dynamics governing
economic exchanges, statistical mechanics provides a useful framework for
studying the collective behavior of monetary systems. By treating agents as
interacting particles and money as a conserved quantity, this work showed how
simple agent-based models can reproduce characteristic equilibrium
distributions.
