\section*{Abstract}

Econophysics is a field that applies concepts from statistical mechanics to economic systems, particularly in understanding money distribution among interacting agents. 
This field employs agent based simulations to validate theoretical predictions, providing insights into the dynamics of economic systems through the lens of statistical mechanics.

The first basic assumption is that money is a conserved quantity during transactions, similar to energy in physical systems.
The equilibrium distribution of money can be derived by maximizing Gibbs entropy under the constraint of total money conservation.
Three different models of money distribution are considered: one without debt, one with debt, and one with proportional money transfers based on how much money each agent is eager to pay.

Then a more complex model is considered, where the wealth of each agent evolves according to a stochastic differential equation, leading to an inverse-gamma distribution of wealth.
