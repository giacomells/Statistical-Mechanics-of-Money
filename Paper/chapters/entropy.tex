\subsection{Entropy evolution}
The entropy of the different models studied in the previous sections
is computed using the formula $S = -\sum_i P_i \ln P_i$, where $P_i$ is the probability of finding an agent with money $m_i$.
In Fig.~\ref{fig:EntropyComparison}, there's an example of entropy evolution for different models with same initial conditions.
It is possible to notice that the model that, in this scenario, reaches its own stationary solution~(entropy is maximized) first is the proportional money transfer model.
The other two models follow, with the basic money distribution model and finally the money distribution with debt model.
